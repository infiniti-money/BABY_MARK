# Photo TTL 90 days and default remembered login
New photos use a shared 90-day TTL for KV expiration and independent authorized read ceiling. Existing inventory expiration remains authoritative; no rewrite or recovery of old photos. Keep 900 MB budget, 200 KB per-photo cap, eviction policy and two-year D1 policy unchanged.
