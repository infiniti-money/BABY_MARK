# RC payroll audit — scope and decisions
- Review: reports.mjs, payroll.mjs, payroll-components.mjs, payroll-attendance.mjs, admin/payroll.js, reports.js, payroll-workbook.mjs and report-documents.mjs.
- Actual time can overlap across non-overlapping scheduled shifts. Detect overlaps in the month plus one adjacent work date on each side; block closing, list pairs, preserve raw and approved times. Draft values remain provisional; don't automatically deduct or assign overlapping hours. Correct and approve time through existing workflow. Extreme legacy intervals beyond supported capture windows may need separate audit.
- CSV now includes pending count, month, draft/closing revision and source epoch. Excel overview flags overlapping hours. Old snapshots remain unmodified; no historical recalculation.
- Golden cases cover hourly, unit, monthly, approved OT, stale approval and missing input. Month-edge overlap test verifies close rejection.
- Current policy: ordinary completed eligible shift = 1 unit, full-day BM-ALLDAY = 2 only when covered within grace, else evidence-bound review. Hourly base uses scheduled intersection, OT uses minutes after scheduled end, only explicit approved minutes paid at configured rate. Early arrival outside scheduled hours is not automatically paid. Actual/regular/extra minutes are distinct; no invented break, holiday, statutory premium, tax, insurance or deduction rules. HR must validate current policy and applicable law. Approval is monthly quantity, not per-shift evidence-bound; equal-duration changes may require manual review. Outside GPS remains review case and no automatic credit, including approved correction; needs explicit business decision. Report actual-time totals may include pending correction records while payroll excludes them; don't confuse actual report with payable totals.
- Migration 0021 is employee CV, not payroll. Production SQL must be applied by an authorized operator, after backup; no production write, deployment, rate change or paid resource here.
